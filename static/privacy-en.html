Weekly Pick Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 3, 2026

한국어 · English

Dabi ("we", "us") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws, and publishes this Privacy Policy to explain how information is handled. This policy applies to the mobile application Weekly Pick (the "App").

1. Our collection principle

The App has no sign-up and no login, and we do not directly collect personal information that identifies you, such as your name, email address, or phone number. However, the information below may be processed automatically in order to serve ads and deliver push notifications.

2. Information processed and why

ItemCollected byPurposeRetention
Advertising ID Google AdMob Serving personalized / non-personalized ads and frequency capping Per Google's policy
Device push token (FCM registration token) Google Firebase Sending content update notifications (only if you allow notifications) Until you turn notifications off or delete the App
App settings (categories, notification preference, tier, etc.) The App (stored on your device) Preserving your preferences Stored only on your device; removed when the App is deleted
Device and network information, crash data Google Ad delivery, service stability and error diagnostics Per Google's policy

App settings are kept only in your device's internal storage and are not transmitted to our servers. Content is delivered as encrypted files, and no personal information is collected in that process.

3. Sharing with third parties and processing on our behalf

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. To provide advertising and notifications, we use the services below, and those providers' own privacy policies apply as well.

  • Google AdMob (advertising): Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications): Firebase Privacy
  • How advertising data is handled: How Google uses data from partner sites

4. Your rights and how to exercise them

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID: go to Settings > Google > Ads on your device to reset the advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads.
  • Turn off push notifications: use the App's settings screen or your device's per-app notification settings at any time.
  • Delete all data: deleting the App removes all settings and cached data stored on your device.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not primarily directed to children under the age of 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
